## Winning Patterns

### Aggressive Price Anchoring & Square Format (high confidence)
Top performers utilize 'aspect_ratio: square' and lead with specific, low-barrier price points or free offers in the 'hook_text' and 'price_visible' fields, whereas bottom performers hide pricing.
*Evidence: lenskart_09, lenskart_05*

### Vibrant Color Contrast Hooks (medium confidence)
Winning ads use high-contrast 'dominant_colors' like orange or light green against neutral backgrounds to create a 'pattern_interrupt: true' visual, while bottom ads use more muted or generic blue/white palettes.
*Evidence: lenskart_09, lenskart_02*

### Celebrity & Status Signaling (medium confidence)
The presence of 'has_celebrity: true' or high-status aspirational copy tied to specific low price points (₹99) drives higher performance than generic functional benefits.
*Evidence: lenskart_05*

## Losing Patterns

### Portrait Format Friction (high confidence)
Consistently, ads with 'aspect_ratio: portrait' appear in the bottom performers, suggesting the creative layout or platform placement for this format is not resonating as well as square assets.
*Evidence: lenskart_08, lenskart_10, lenskart_07*

### Generic Aspirational Hooks (high confidence)
Bottom performers rely on the exact same 'hook_text' ('Built for the bold. Designed for the driven.') which lacks a specific 'value_proposition' or 'discount_present', leading to lower engagement.
*Evidence: lenskart_08, lenskart_07*

### Functional Overload (medium confidence)
Ads focusing on 'tone: functional' and solving technical problems (foggy lenses) without a strong financial 'offer' or 'discount_type' underperform compared to style-led ads.
*Evidence: lenskart_10*

## Brand Signature
Lenskart's creative style is defined by high-saturation, product-focused lifestyle photography that emphasizes color-coordinated frames against clean, minimalist backgrounds. They rely on a mix of aspirational 'boardroom' messaging and aggressive 'Buy 1 Get 1' or entry-level pricing hooks to drive conversion.

## Biggest Gap
Complete absence of 'has_ugc', 'has_reviews', or 'has_ratings' across all 10 ads, missing a massive opportunity to build trust through social proof.

## Test Ideas

### 1. The ₹99 Unboxing Review [HIGH]
**Format:** video
**Hook:** A customer unboxing a premium-looking frame with a text overlay saying 'I only paid ₹99 for these.'
**Headline:** Premium Frames. Pocket-Friendly Price.
**Visual brief:** A 1:1 square video featuring a selfie-style unboxing. The bottom third of the frame should feature a persistent '₹99' price tag in a high-contrast orange bubble.
**Hypothesis:** Addressing the 'Biggest Gap' (UGC/Social Proof) while leveraging 'Pattern 1' (Price Anchoring) will build the trust necessary to convert low-price offers.

### 2. Vibrant Celeb Status Pop [HIGH]
**Format:** static
**Hook:** A celebrity wearing neon-framed glasses against a high-contrast vibrant orange background.
**Headline:** Look Like a Star for Just ₹999.
**Visual brief:** Square format featuring a high-saturation orange background. Use a celebrity image with high-status styling, ensuring the eyewear color (e.g., light green) creates a 'pattern_interrupt' against the background.
**Hypothesis:** Combines 'Pattern 2' (Vibrant Color Contrast) with 'Pattern 3' (Celebrity Signaling) to maximize scroll-stop and aspirational value.

### 3. 5-Star Price Anchor Carousel [HIGH]
**Format:** carousel
**Hook:** Large 5-star rating stars appearing over the first frame's product with '1M+ Happy Customers' text.
**Headline:** India's Highest Rated Eyewear — From ₹99
**Visual brief:** 1:1 square cards. Card 1 shows a best-selling frame with a '4.9/5' star badge and '₹99' price anchor. Subsequent cards show different styles with short 1-line customer testimonials.
**Hypothesis:** Directly solves the 'Biggest Gap' (Social Proof) while maintaining the winning 'Pattern 1' (Square format & aggressive price anchoring).

### 4. Neon Price Interrupt [MEDIUM]
**Format:** static
**Hook:** Large, bold 'FREE' or '₹0' text in light green on a dark neutral background.
**Headline:** Your First Frame is on Us!
**Visual brief:** Square layout with a minimalist shot of a premium frame centered. Surround the frame with a vibrant light green geometric border that pops against a dark grey background to draw eyes to the price.
**Hypothesis:** Uses 'Pattern 2' (Color Contrast) to draw immediate attention to the 'Pattern 1' (Aggressive Price Anchor) of the 'Free' offer.

### 5. CEO Look for Less (UGC) [MEDIUM]
**Format:** video
**Hook:** Creator says 'How I look like a CEO on a budget' while putting on Lenskart glasses.
**Headline:** The Boardroom Look. Starting at ₹999.
**Visual brief:** A 'Get Ready With Me' style video where a creator transitions from casual to professional. Include a 'Verified Buyer' badge and a '4.8 Star' overlay in the top right corner.
**Hypothesis:** Merges 'Pattern 3' (Status Signaling/Boardroom messaging) with the 'Biggest Gap' (UGC/Social Proof) to make the aspirational lifestyle feel attainable and trustworthy.
